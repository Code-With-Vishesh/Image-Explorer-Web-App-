Here are feature ideas to enhance your Image Explorer:

## 🔍 **Search & Discovery**
- **Advanced filters**: Resolution, color, orientation (portrait/landscape), date range
- **Search suggestions**: Auto-complete based on popular searches 
- **Trending images**: Show what's popular today
- **Similar images**: Find visually similar images to ones you like
- **Saved searches**: Quick access to frequently used searches

## 💾 **Collections & Organization**
- **Favorites/Wishlist**: Save images to personal collections
- **Create albums**: Organize images into custom folders
- **Tags**: Add custom tags to organize images
- **Notes**: Add descriptions to saved images
- **Smart collections**: Auto-organize by date uploaded, source, etc.

## 👥 **Social Features**
- **Share collections**: Create shareable links to your image sets
- **Public profiles**: Show your uploaded images to others
- **Rating system**: Like and rate images
- **Comments**: Add feedback on images
- **Follow users**: Follow other image explorers

## 🎨 **Image Editing**
- **Crop & resize**: Built-in editor for quick modifications
- **Filters**: Apply vintage, grayscale, brightness adjustments
- **Watermark**: Add your watermark before download
- **Batch download**: Download multiple images at once
- **Format selection**: Download as JPG, PNG, WebP

## 📊 **Analytics & Stats**
- **Download history**: Track what you've downloaded
- **Search stats**: See your most searched terms
- **Usage dashboard**: View your activity over time
- **Image statistics**: Resolution, size, file format info

## ⚡ **Performance & UX**
- **Infinite scroll**: Auto-load more images as you scroll
- **Image comparison**: Side-by-side view of two images
- **Lightbox improvements**: Keyboard navigation (arrow keys), zoom
- **Dark/Light mode toggle**: Better for different times
- **Offline mode**: Cache images for offline browsing

## 🔐 **Account Features**
- **Social login**: Google, GitHub, Facebook sign-in
- **Email verification**: Secure account creation
- **Password reset**: Forgot password option
- **Profile settings**: Customize username, avatar, preferences
- **Export data**: Download all your saved metadata

## 🌍 **Advanced Discovery**
- **Location-based search**: Images from specific places
- **License filtering**: Filter by usage rights (CC0, CC-BY, etc.)
- **Video search**: Extend to stock videos (Pexels has video API)
- **Image categories**: Browse by curated categories
- **Seasonal content**: Holiday/seasonal image collections

## 📱 **Mobile & Responsive**
- **PWA support**: Install as app on phone
- **Mobile upload**: Take photos directly from camera
- **Share to social**: Quick share to Instagram, Pinterest
- **Mobile notification**: Get alerts on new faves

## 🛠️ **Admin/Maintenance**
- **User dashboard**: View account activity
- **Storage quota**: Track usage limits
- **API usage**: Show Pexels/Pixabay API usage stats
- **Feedback form**: Let users report issues

## 🎯 **Gamification**
- **Achievement badges**: Unlock badges for activities
- **Streaks**: Daily search or upload streaks
- **Leaderboards**: Most active uploaders
- **Points system**: Earn points for activities

## 🔗 **Integration**
